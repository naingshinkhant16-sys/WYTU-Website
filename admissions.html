Undergraduate Admissions

Join West Yangon Technological University and begin your journey toward a professional degree in engineering or architecture.

Overview

Admission Process Overview

Undergraduate admission to West Yangon Technological University is conducted in accordance with the national higher education admissions guidelines prescribed by the Ministry of Education, Myanmar.

Admissions are competitive and evaluated based on candidate marks in the national matriculation examination, with specific subject requirements in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and English.

Notice on Official Dates & Cut-off Scores

Official application deadlines, minimum score cut-offs, and department quotas for the upcoming academic session are announced directly through official Ministry channels.

ℹ️ Official admission schedule information will be updated soon.
Requirements

General Admission Requirements

Qualifications necessary for admission to WYTU engineering degree programs.

Matriculation Certificate

Successful completion of the Myanmar National Matriculation Examination (High School Completion Certificate) with science stream subjects.

Subject Specialization

Strong performance in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and English subjects in the matriculation examination.

Centralized Allocation

Application submission and university department allocation conducted through the Higher Education Entrance System.

Steps

Application Steps

How candidates apply for admission at WYTU.

1

Review Requirements

Check matriculation exam results and subject score requirements.

2

Submit Application

Submit university preference form to the Department of Higher Education.

3

Allocation Announcement

Check university selection lists released by the Ministry.

4

Campus Registration

Complete student enrollment registration at WYTU campus.

Required Documents for Campus Enrollment

  • 📁 Original & attested copies of Matriculation Examination Mark Sheet.
  • 📁 National Scrutiny Card (NRC) copy or student identification copy.
  • 📁 Recent passport-size photographs of applicant.
  • 📁 Official admission allocation letter issued by the Ministry of Education.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of undergraduate engineering programs at WYTU?

All Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) and Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.) degree programs at WYTU span 6 academic years.

Can students change their department after admission?

Department transfer requests are governed strictly by Ministry of Education regulations and academic performance regulations.

Where can I get official assistance regarding enrollment?

Official student affairs contact details and campus office hours will be updated soon on the official website.
