About WYTU

West Yangon Technological University

West Yangon Technological University (WYTU) is a premier higher education technological institution located in Hlaing Tharyar Township, Yangon, Myanmar. WYTU is dedicated to educating the next generation of engineers, architects, and technologists.

With 11 core engineering degree programs and 4 supportive academic departments, WYTU emphasizes rigorous scientific theory, practical laboratory experimentation, and industrial innovation.

Admissions

Admissions & Entry Process

WYTU welcomes qualified high school graduates and matriculated students across Myanmar. Selection is based on academic merit in the national matriculation examination with a strong emphasis on Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and English.

  • ✔️ National Matriculation Examination Qualification
  • ✔️ Science & Mathematics Emphasis
  • ✔️ Centralized Ministry Admission Allocation
Explore Admissions →

Admissions Information Notice
