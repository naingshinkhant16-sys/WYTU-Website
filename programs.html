Academic Degree Programs

Comprehensive 6-year professional engineering and architectural degree programs at West Yangon Technological University.

Curriculum

Undergraduate Degree Programs

All degree programs are accredited 6-year full-time university curriculums combining classroom instruction, laboratory experimentation, field surveying, and final-year capstone engineering project design.

B.E. (CEIT)

Computer Engineering & IT

Software development, algorithms, computer networks, database design, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and embedded IoT systems.

Learn More →
B.E. (Civil)

Civil Engineering

Structural analysis, reinforced concrete design, soil mechanics, surveying, transportation engineering, and water resources management.

Learn More →
B.E. (EP)

Electrical Power Engineering

Power grid generation, transmission, electrical machines, high voltage engineering, smart power systems, and solar energy technology.

Learn More →
B.E. (EC)

Electrical & Communication Eng.

Electronic circuit design, wireless telecommunications, microwave antennas, fiber optic networks, and digital signal processing.

Learn More →
B.E. (Mechatronics)

Mechatronics Engineering

Industrial robotics, PLC automation, sensors and actuators, microcontrollers, pneumatic controls, and mechatronic design.

Learn More →
B.Arch.

Architecture

Architectural design studios, building technology, urban planning, landscape design, CAD modeling, and building environmental systems.

Learn More →
B.E. (Chemical)

Chemical Engineering

Chemical process design, thermodynamics, reaction kinetics, separation operations, fluid flow, and bioprocess technology.

Learn More →
B.E. (Met)

Metallurgical & Material Science

Physical metallurgy, metal extraction, phase diagrams, material characterization, heat treatment, and composite materials.

Learn More →
B.E. (Textile)

Textile Engineering

Fiber science, yarn spinning technology, weaving/knitting mechanisms, textile dyeing, printing, and garment manufacturing.

Learn More →
B.E. (Mining)

Mechanical Engineering

Surface and underground mining methods, rock mechanics, drilling and blasting, mine ventilation, and environmental reclamation.

Learn More →
B.E. (Agri)

Agricultural Engineering

Farm machinery design, irrigation engineering, soil and water conservation, post-harvest grain storage, and precision agriculture.
