Scholarships & Student Financial Aid

Recognizing academic merit and providing financial support to empower engineering and architecture students at WYTU.

Financial Support

Scholarship Opportunities

West Yangon Technological University offers academic merit awards, government stipends, and industry-partnered grants.

🎖️

University Merit Scholarship

Awarded annually to top academic performers across each of the 15 academic departments based on annual GPA examination results.

Category: Academic Merit
🤝

Student Assistance Stipend

Need-based monthly living and tuition aid provided to deserving engineering candidates requiring financial assistance.

Category: Need-Based Assistance
🔬

Industry Research Project Grant

Sponsored by engineering technology firms for final-year students conducting innovative capstone design research.

Category: Research & Capstone
Eligibility

Scholarship Eligibility Criteria

  • ✔️ Enrolled as a full-time student in an official WYTU undergraduate degree program.
  • ✔️ Maintained exemplary academic attendance and good standing in department courses.
  • ✔️ Demonstrated high academic standing or verified need for financial aid support.

Application Deadlines & Schedule

Scholarship application windows are opened at the start of each academic semester. Applications are processed through the Student Affairs Office.
